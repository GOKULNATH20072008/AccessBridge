One-Switch Scanning Demo

Use the widget in the top-right corner. Turn on scanning, pick a speed, then drive the page with a single switch key (Space by default). The highlight advances automatically; press the switch to activate.

Two modes, in the settings panel: Auto (highlight moves by itself, one switch activates) and Step (two switches: Space moves to the next item, Enter activates it). Press Esc anytime to stop scanning. A status pill next to the controls shows the current mode and position; screen readers are told what happened on start, stop, edit, and activation.
